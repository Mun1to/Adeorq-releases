## changelog.0.titulo
Discord with nothing to configure, and a bin for sessions

## changelog.0.texto
Discord activity works without registering any application and speaks the language of the app.
Sessions go to a bin instead of vanishing, panes can no longer be dragged narrower than their
own header, and the Foreman can change one setting across every terminal at once.

## changelog.1.titulo
The terminal squad

## changelog.1.texto
Open as many sessions of a project as you want in one go and they lay themselves out in a grid.
Plus a redrawn logo and a fix for the sideways scroll that crept into the window.

## changelog.2.titulo
The Agenda, with a calendar

## changelog.2.texto
A new tab for ideas and next steps, calendar included. Projects can be renamed without touching
their folders, each one shows its logo on the card, and the icons and buttons you used to guess
at are now visible.
