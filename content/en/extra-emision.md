# Broadcast mode · proposed block, not built · English

Optional block for a second pass, id `#emision`, between Features and Download.

## emision.eyebrow
For live streams

## emision.titulo
Share your screen without giving away your keys.

## emision.lead
One button covers paths, session names and your email, and screens whatever the agents print
before it is painted: anything shaped like a key comes out as dots. The agent still receives the
real text, the screen does not.

## emision.item1
Solid covers, not blur: a blur can be undone from the recording

## emision.item2
Hold Alt to peek, let go and it covers again

## emision.item3
A full screen curtain on a shortcut, and it raises itself if a key is caught

## emision.nota
What it does not do, said plainly: it cannot fix what was already on screen before you turned it
on, it does not cover your browser or your Windows notifications, and no list of patterns is
ever complete. A 30 second delay in OBS still protects you more than anything else.
