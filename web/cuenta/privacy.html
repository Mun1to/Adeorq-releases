Privacy

Last updated: [[FALTA: publication date]]

Adeorq works the same without an account. This is what happens to your data if you choose to create one.

Who handles it

[[FALTA: full name or company name]], tax ID [[FALTA: NIF]], based at [[FALTA: address]]. For anything on this page: [[FALTA: contact email]].

What we keep

  • Your email and your username.
  • Your password, only in a hashed form nobody can read.
  • The dates you signed up and last signed in.
  • Your IP address and your browser for each session.

You need them to have an account: without an email, a password and a name, one cannot be created.

What for, and on what basis

  • Running your account and holding your name, and sending the emails to confirm and to reset your password. It is what you ask for when you create it: performance of a contract (GDPR article 6.1.b).
  • Protecting accounts from misuse and abuse, which is what the IP, the browser and the attempt limits are for. This is a legitimate interest (GDPR article 6.1.f), and you can object to it.

We do not use your data for advertising, send newsletters or build profiles, and no decision about you is made automatically.

Who else sees it

  • Supabase (Supabase Pte. Ltd., Singapore) keeps the database and handles sign-in. The data is stored in Ireland.
  • Cloudflare serves this site, and sees your IP when you visit.
  • [[FALTA: email provider]] sends the emails to confirm and to reset.

They work on our behalf. Since some are outside the European Economic Area, those transfers rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and, for Cloudflare, also on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. You can ask us for a copy of those safeguards.

How long

  • As long as you have the account. You can delete it whenever you like from your account, with your password, and it leaves the database right then.
  • If you do not confirm your email, 48 hours. After that the account deletes itself.
  • The provider's backups may keep it for up to [[FALTA: Supabase backup period]].

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or take your data, to limit its use, or to object, by writing to the email above. If you think we got it wrong, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency.

Age

You must be at least 14 years old to create an account.

Cookies

This page sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Your session lasts as long as the tab stays open.
